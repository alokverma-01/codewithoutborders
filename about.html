What I focus on

Every project starts from the same place: understand what the site actually needs to do, then build it with modern tools, clean structure and no unnecessary weight.

That means responsive layouts that hold up from a small phone to a large desktop monitor, interfaces that feel considered rather than templated, and code that's organized enough to maintain and extend later.

The name reflects the approach: borders — of geography, of device, of team size — shouldn't limit what a website can be.
