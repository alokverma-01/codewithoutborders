A note on payment security

This page only displays public payment identifiers and links — never a secret key. A payment is only confirmed once it's verified on the server side (for example, by a Razorpay webhook or a manual check against your bank/PayPal activity). This page never claims a payment succeeded on its own. If a payment fails or is cancelled on the provider's side, please try again or reach out on WhatsApp.
